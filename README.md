**A private women's health companion for Indian women. Runs entirely on your phone.**

## What is Sakhi?

Sakhi (सखी) means *friend* — the kind you can ask anything without feeling judged.

Many women have health questions they can't comfortably ask at home, at a clinic, or online. Sakhi answers them in your own language, on your own phone, without sending a single word to a server.

## What Sakhi helps with

| 🤰 Pregnancy & Baby | Pregnancy care, postpartum recovery, breastfeeding, JSY & PMMVY schemes |
| 🧠 Mind & Emotions | Stress, anxiety, low mood, sleep, domestic violence support |
| 💪 Body & Fitness | Exercise during periods, yoga for PCOS, home workouts, body positivity |
| 🥗 Food & Nutrition | Anaemia, iron and calcium, PCOS diet, Indian food remedies |

## Private by design

- **No account, no sign-up**
- **Your chats, period logs and notes stay on your phone** — nothing is uploaded, ever
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking**
- **Works offline** — the AI runs on your device, not in the cloud

The first time you open a chat, Sakhi downloads its AI model once (about 2.6 GB, on WiFi or mobile data). Only the model comes down; nothing goes up. If your daily data runs out, the download pauses and resumes from where it stopped.

Read the full [Privacy Policy](https://mithilesh-lala.github.io/sakhi-privacy/).

## Requirements

| | |
|---|---|
| Android | 7.0 or newer |
| RAM | 4 GB or more recommended (3 GB works, but chat is slower) |
| Free storage | About 3 GB for the one-time AI model download |

Period Tracker and Helplines work on any supported phone, even without the AI download.

> Sakhi runs a full AI model on your phone, so replies can take a minute or two on mid-range devices. That's the price of keeping everything private.

## Important

Sakhi gives general health information. It is **not a doctor** and does not diagnose or prescribe. For any health concern, please see a doctor or your ASHA didi.

**In an emergency, call 112. Women's Helpline: 181.**
